About me

Green Roots Community Garden is a registered non-profit that has been operating and backed by more than 30 years of experience based on achieving environmental justice ,Green Roots initiative has been set to restore the acres of urban salt marshes and has served to create parks which increased access to the general public whilst improving the quality of water within Chelsea creek , the organisation id focused on building up the community work that serve the purpose of investing into the future of Chelsea and east Boston ,the organisation was founded in 1994 and was considere as a grassroot committee and then in 2016 it was decided upon to be an independent non-profit organization and the founder and leader was Rosean Bongiovanni who was a native of Chelsea,Rooted in community,growing for change a city where evry community has access to fresh,locally grown products,clean water and healthy waterfront spaces that serve to foster cultural and economic diversity
